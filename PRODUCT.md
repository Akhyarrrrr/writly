# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Writers who want to publish under a public identity and readers who discover work from multiple authors.

## Product Purpose

Make long-form writing safe to draft, deliberate to publish, and easy to discover by author, topic, and search.

## Brand Personality

Focused, literary, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS decoration, unsafe one-click publishing, and editor controls that hide their meaning.

## Design Principles

- Protect the writer's work before adding publishing features.
- Make draft, review, and published states unmistakable.
- Keep author identity and article discovery visible.
- Prefer familiar, accessible editing controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete authoring, visible focus, reduced motion, and editable image alternatives.
